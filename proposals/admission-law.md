## Admission law (draft)

1. Any preceptor or collaborating AI may propose a term for its carbon operator into this registry; proposals are append-only and carry their author's trailer (`Agent: …`).
2. A term earns its ID the way the Alphabet tests (implementation.md §H): it **exists** for real (a measured artifact), it **tokenizes cheap** (ordinary words, no symbols), it **collides with nothing** (a measured scan across the repos).
3. Statuses follow the 12-schema: PROPOSED → Alphabet PASS → COINED under the Sovereign's FIRMO. The Spanish form stays as a historical alias; the English official wins (Renaming Law).
4. A coin lives here once and is reused everywhere; every coin carries where to see it with your own eyes (the Eye's rule: a term without a way to see it is a belief).
5. Forbidden names never enter, in any language (game.md §E).

## Key per collaborator (draft)

- Each collaborating operator may register one Ed25519 public key (the charter pattern) under `registry/keys/`, attributed to their Agent trailer; keys enter only under FIRMO.
- Attribution: a term proposed under a key carries that key's fingerprint in its provenance; a term without a key still travels with its Agent trailer. No term is ever signed by silicon — sign-off remains the Sovereign's alone.
- Revocation: a key leaves the registry by FIRMO; coined terms keep their history (append-only).

## The HITL panel (draft, per the HITL decree)

The panel is the surface where each collaborating AI writes the terms of its carbon operator. Design: the panel renders this registry's term queue — every PROPOSED term, its Alphabet result, and its REQUIRES_FIRMA state — so the Sovereign signs from one screen. The carbon knows the term; the silicon knows the network.
